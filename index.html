Electricity reliability

Blackout — Chasing the Light

1985–2024 calibrationto 2035 projection
Model built · research development

Studies Iran's widening electricity supply–demand gap and recurring blackouts as an endogenous outcome of interacting demand, production, available capacity, finance, tariffs, fuel constraints, regional imbalance, political pressure, and investment response.

  • distinguishes installed capacity from actually available/reliable supply
  • endogenizes renewable-policy pressure from system conditions
  • supports policy scenarios around investment, tariffs, demand management, and generation choices
Distributed water systems

DWD — Distributed Water Desalination

NorthCenterSouth
Model built · public summary

Explores distributed, often solar-powered desalination near demand centers as a water-resilience strategy and asks when local supply can outperform continued centralized expansion.

  • links desalination capacity, distributed water supply, renewable energy interaction, cost, adoption, leakage exposure, groundwater pressure, and resilience
  • examines grid stress, conveyance losses, regional suitability, and policy barriers
  • tests potential trade-offs between decentralized supply and centralized infrastructure lock-in
Water infrastructure

Water Infrastructure Choices

2015–20403 zones9 stress scenarios + BAU
WSC 2026 research

A zonal simulation study of infrastructure choices under interacting climate, population, leakage, investment, construction-delay, and policy pressures in Iran.

  • zones: North/Northwest, Central Inland, Coastal South
  • strategies: leakage-first, centralized expansion, distributed/local desalination, adaptive portfolios
  • metrics: unmet demand, total cost, cost per effective m³ delivered, resilience/recovery performance

Modeling approach

How I use System Dynamics

Feedback
Reinforcing and balancing loops explain why interventions can amplify or self-limit.
Stocks & flows
Capacity, infrastructure, water, knowledge, participation, and other accumulations are explicit.
Delays
Construction, policy response, deterioration, learning, and behavioral adaptation are not instantaneous.
Policy learning
Models are used to test robustness, unintended consequences, and changing dominant feedback.
