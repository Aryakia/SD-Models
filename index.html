How I use models

Feedback
I focus on reinforcing and balancing feedback structures that generate system behavior.
Accumulation & delay
Stocks, flows, infrastructure lifetimes, implementation delays, and path dependence are treated explicitly.
Policy learning
Models are used to test why systems behave as they do and where interventions may create unintended consequences.
